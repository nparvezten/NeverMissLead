instance's data, and do not control what content a business owner uploads

## 3. Data Handling & Privacy Responsibility
Each deployment of NeverMissLead is operated by the business (or developer)
who installs it. Operators agree to the following:
* **Lawful Basis for Contact Data**: Operators are responsible for ensuring
  they have a lawful basis (e.g., visitor consent at point of submission)
  to store and contact leads captured through the widget, consistent with
  applicable law (e.g., India's DPDP Act 2023, or equivalent regulation in
  their jurisdiction).
* **Encryption Baseline**: Lead PII (name, phone, email) handled by the
  built-in data layer is structured via AES-256 field encryption. Operators
  extending the schema with additional PII fields are responsible for
  applying equivalent protection.
* **No Public Unauthenticated Lookup**: This software must not be deployed
  in a configuration that exposes captured lead or conversation data via an
  unauthenticated public endpoint.
* **Retention & Deletion**: Operators are responsible for honoring
  deletion/export requests from their own leads in line with applicable
  data-protection law; the software provides the data-model primitives to
  support this but does not itself constitute legal compliance.

## 4. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer
Responses generated by the RAG/LLM pipeline are produced automatically
from the operator's uploaded content and a third-party language model
provider. While the system is designed to cite sources and abstain when
uncertain, **AI-generated responses may occasionally be inaccurate.**
Operators are responsible for reviewing their knowledge base content for
accuracy and for monitoring flagged/handoff conversations. This software
is not a substitute for direct communication on matters requiring legal,
financial, medical, or safety-critical accuracy.
