# Skyline Realty Partners — Official FAQ & Client Advisory Guide 2026

## 1. About Our Agency
Skyline Realty Partners is a full-service residential and commercial real estate brokerage. With over $250M in closed transaction volume across the metropolitan area, our licensed brokers provide data-driven advisory for buyers, sellers, tenants, and real estate investors.

## 2. Real Estate Services Offered
- **Buyer Representation**: Personalized property searches, private tours, comparative market valuation, offer drafting, and escrow negotiation.
- **Seller Listings**: Comprehensive market analysis (CMA), high-definition HDR photography, 3D Matterport virtual tours, MLS syndication, and open house hosting.
- **Luxury & Residential Rentals**: Tenant placement, background/credit screening, lease agreement drafting, and move-in inspection.
- **Commercial & Office Leasing**: Tenant and landlord representation for retail, medical, and corporate office spaces.
- **Investment Advisory**: Multi-family property underwriting, cap rate analysis, and 1031 tax-deferred exchange guidance.

## 3. Commission Rates & Fee Structure
- **Residential Property Sales**: Standard **5% total sales commission** (typically structured as 2.5% for the listing brokerage and 2.5% for the buyer's brokerage). For dual agency or bundled transactions, competitive reduced rates are available.
- **Buyer Representation Fee**: In standard residential transactions, the buyer's agent fee is paid by the seller out of closing proceeds; no direct out-of-pocket broker fee is charged to the buyer.
- **Rental Tenant Placement**: Flat fee equivalent to **one month's rent**, payable upon successful lease execution and security deposit escrow.
- **Home Valuation Consultation**: 100% complimentary, no-obligation comparative market analysis (CMA) report.

## 4. Service Areas & Neighborhoods
- **Primary Metro Neighborhoods**: Downtown Core, Financial District, Waterfront Promenade, Uptown Arts District.
- **Suburban & Residential Communities**: North Bay Suburbs, Eastridge Hills, Oakwood Pines, Westlake Valley.
- We serve a 35-mile radius covering both primary urban centers and surrounding suburban commuter school districts.

## 5. Property Viewing & Tour Scheduling
- Private accompanied viewings require a minimum of **24 hours' advance notice** to coordinate with current occupants and listing agents.
- Weekend open houses are conducted every Saturday and Sunday from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM for all active exclusive listings.
- Virtual live video walkthroughs (FaceTime / Zoom) can be scheduled for out-of-state or international relocating buyers.

## 6. Required Offer Documents & Closing Timeline
- **Required Offer Documents**: To submit a formal purchase offer on any property, buyers must provide a verified mortgage pre-approval letter (or bank statement proof of funds for cash offers) and a copy of government-issued photo identification.
- **Listing to Closing Timeline**: The average timeline from an accepted purchase offer to keys-in-hand closing is **30 to 45 calendar days** (allowing standard periods for home inspection, appraisal, and mortgage underwriting). Cash transactions can close in as few as **10 to 14 days**.

## 7. Agency Representation & Cancellation Policy
- Our standard exclusive listing agreement is for **90 days**.
- If a client is unsatisfied for any reason, we offer an **Unconditional Satisfaction Guarantee**: agreements can be cancelled with 7 days' written notice with zero termination penalty.
