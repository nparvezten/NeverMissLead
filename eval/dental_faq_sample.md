# Bright Smile Dental Clinic — Official FAQ & Services Guide 2026

## 1. About Our Clinic
Bright Smile Dental Clinic provides comprehensive general, cosmetic, and pediatric dental care. Our clinical team consists of licensed dental surgeons, periodontists, and orthodontists with over 15 years of combined clinical excellence.

## 2. Dental Services Offered
- **Preventive Care**: Routine dental cleaning, comprehensive oral examinations, digital low-dose X-rays, fluoride treatment, and dental sealants.
- **Restorative Dentistry**: Composite tooth-colored fillings, porcelain crowns, bridges, and pain-free root canal therapy.
- **Orthodontics**: Invisalign clear aligners, ceramic braces, traditional metal braces, and post-treatment retainers.
- **Cosmetic Procedures**: Professional in-office laser teeth whitening, take-home custom whitening trays, and porcelain veneers.
- **Pediatric Dentistry**: Child-friendly exams, gentle cleanings, habit counseling (thumb-sucking), and space maintainers.
- **Oral Surgery**: Simple and surgical tooth extractions, wisdom teeth removal, and bone grafting.

## 3. Pricing & Fee Schedule
- **Routine Dental Cleaning & Examination**: $120 (includes full polish, plaque removal, and oral cancer screening).
- **Digital X-Ray Series (Bitewing/Panoramic)**: $65 (included complimentary with first comprehensive exam).
- **Composite Tooth-Colored Filling**: $180 to $250 per tooth depending on the number of surfaces treated.
- **Single-Visit Porcelain Crown**: $950 per unit.
- **In-Office Laser Teeth Whitening**: $350 (includes 1-hour laser session + custom maintenance gel).
- **Invisalign Clear Aligners**: Complete treatment packages start from $3,800 following a free initial 3D digital scan.
- **Emergency Examination & Diagnostic X-Ray**: $95 flat fee.

## 4. Insurance & Payment Options
- We are in-network preferred providers for **Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian, Aetna, and United Concordia**.
- For uninsured patients, we offer our **Bright Smile Dental Savings Plan** ($199/year for 2 free cleanings, free X-rays, and 20% off all procedures).
- Flexible zero-interest monthly financing is available through **CareCredit** and **LendingClub Patient Solutions** (up to 24 months).

## 6. Appointment Booking & Cancellation Policy
- Appointments can be booked 24/7 online through our patient portal or by calling our front desk during clinic hours.
- We require at least **24 hours' notice** for appointment rescheduling or cancellation to avoid a $50 late cancellation fee.

## 7. Emergency Dental Care Policy
- For acute dental emergencies (severe toothache, knocked-out tooth, broken crown, facial swelling), we reserve dedicated daily emergency slots.
- Active patients experiencing after-hours emergencies should call our clinic line and press **1** to be connected directly to the on-call dentist.
